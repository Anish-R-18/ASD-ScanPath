TPP-GAZE ASD/TD - MAIN FINDINGS
(Only results directly supported by the experiments. Categories kept distinct.)

SUMMARY
Group conditioning gave a small in-domain likelihood gain that did not carry over to corrected
Gaze4ASD: unconditioned Phase 1 is better overall there. Real ASD and TD children differed in spatial
gaze measures. Generated scanpaths reproduce some of those differences, too weakly, and get others
wrong. Temperature 1 improves external spatial behaviour but does not fix timing.

NLL is per fixation event; lower is better. Gaze4ASD results use the fit-to-height mapping with Policy A,
which is strongly supported but partly inferred, and NOT experimentally confirmed.

E) TEMPERATURE (inference-time setting; the model and its likelihood do not change)
   Mechanism: each spatial mixture sigma becomes sigma^tau, with means and weights unchanged. tau=1 is the
   original distribution. All mixture weight has sigma < 1, so tau=2 narrows spread. Durations are not tempered.
   Selection: tau=1 was selected using Saliency4ASD validation images. Gaze4ASD was not used to choose it.
   External check, paired seeds (per-image mean absolute error, tau=1 vs tau=2; four prespecified tests):
     Saccade amplitude  ASD .0179 vs .0377 (52.5% lower)   TD .0138 vs .0276 (49.9% lower)
     Revisit rate       ASD .0569 vs .1681 (66.1% lower)   TD .0449 vs .1032 (56.5% lower)
     All four: Holm p <= .0005, 95% CIs exclude zero, 10/10 seeds.
   Exploratory: dispersion error is also lower (47.8%, 34.1%). Direction concentration is slightly worse
   (7.6%, 19.2%). Fixation duration, count, rate and total time do not change beyond seed noise.
   Caveat: TD revisit reverses within the real viewing window, so its close match partly depends on excess
   fixations. Amplitude and dispersion results hold in that check.
   Final behavioural generation setting: tau=1. This is not a likelihood improvement.

No clinical validity is claimed. These are computational modelling results on group-labelled gaze data; they
are not a diagnostic or individual-level tool. All statistics are exploratory, except that the four temperature
tests were prespecified (not preregistered).
