SUPERSEDED - DO NOT USE THESE VALUES IN THE FINAL PAPER

Everything in this folder comes from the original Gaze4ASD evaluation, which used an incorrect
coordinate mapping. The files are kept unchanged for reproducibility.

What went wrong
- The original mapping assumed native-size stimuli centred on the 2560x1440 screen.
- A later audit found strong evidence for fit-to-height scaling instead: stimuli scaled to the
  screen height and centred horizontally. This mapping is strongly supported but partly inferred.
  The original draw rectangle and presentation script are not available, so it is not experimentally
  confirmed.

Where the corrected results are
- Tracked summaries: results/external_gaze4asd_v2/ (start with README.md)
- Current findings:  results/final_results/MAIN_FINDINGS.txt
- Full local outputs (not tracked by Git): training/runs/external_gaze4asd_v2/

Superseded values include
- Overall NLL +1.0393 (Policy A) and +0.5283 (Policy B); position NLL +1.44 and +0.91.
- The conclusions "position likelihood does not transfer" and "external spatial domain gap".
- All spatial real-vs-generated comparisons, and "generated spatial scale is about half of real".
- Real saccade amplitude g = +0.46 and spatial dispersion g = +0.65 (corrected: +0.65 and +0.80).
