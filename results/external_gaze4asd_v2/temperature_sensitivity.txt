It changes sampling only, not the model or its NLL. Paths inside these reports refer to local, Git-ignored outputs.

TEMPERATURE SENSITIVITY - FINAL REPORT

Saliency4ASD validation images only (45). No retraining, no test split, no Gaze4ASD. All statistics are
exploratory. Temperature is an inference-time setting: it does not change learned weights.

BOTTOM LINE
tau = 2 does narrow generated gaze. It squares every mixture sigma, which shortens saccades by about 18%
and raises revisit rate by about 50%. tau = 1 is the better overall setting on these images: it matches
amplitude and revisit rate better, and it is the distribution the model's NLL actually scores. tau = 2
looks better on overall dispersion, but mostly because its narrowing offsets the excess fixations.
Neither temperature fixes fixation overproduction or the wrong-direction group differences.

Recommendation: use tau = 1 for behavioural generation, and report tau = 2 as a sensitivity result.
This rests on validation images only and is not confirmed on independent data.
It is not a likelihood improvement: the model's likelihood is the same either way.

D. ANSWERS

1. Which gives more realistic spatial dispersion?
   Not clear. Over full scanpaths tau=1 is too wide (+.014, +.011) and tau=2 too narrow (-.005, -.006); tau=2 is
   numerically closer, in 10/10 and 8/10 seeds. However, the per-image gain includes zero (Holm 1.0), and the
   window-matched check favours tau=1 for ASD and ties for TD. tau=2's advantage is mostly offsetting errors.

2. Which gives more realistic saccade amplitude?
   tau=1. Error +.0003 and +.0062, against -.0248 and -.0162 at tau=2; 10/10 seeds in both groups.
   Clear for ASD (distance Holm .001; bias CI excludes zero). Weaker for TD (distance Holm .097; bias CI
   touches zero; MAE Holm 1.0).

3. Does temperature affect fixation duration?
   No detectable effect. It is not tempered at all. Any route is indirect, through history, and the observed
   changes (at most .001 s) are within seed noise.

4. Does temperature affect revisit rate?
   Yes, strongly: +.11 and +.10 at tau=2 (45/45 images, 10/10 seeds). tau=1 is closer to real over full scanpaths
   (Holm .002, .037). In the window-matched check, ASD still favours tau=1; TD ties.

5. Does either reduce fixation-count overproduction?
   No. Both give about 10.6 (ASD) and 9.9 (TD), against real 6.7 and 7.8. Inside the real window, the excess is
   still about 0.9. Temperature is not a remedy.

6. Which better reproduces real ASD-vs-TD differences?
   tau=1 on amplitude (51% vs 29%) and revisit (22% vs 6%), both still too small. Dispersion is unresolved.
   Duration and rate are unchanged. Fixation count, total time and direction concentration are wrong in both.

7. Are conclusions stable across seeds?
   Yes for direct effects on amplitude, dispersion and revisit (all 10 seeds, effect 11-24 times seed SD), and
   for amplitude and revisit realism (10/10). Dispersion realism is not settled by seeds because the question
   turns on the window, not on noise. Duration and count differences are noise.

8. Is tau=2 artificially narrowing the spatial distribution?
   Yes. This is a direct result of the formula, because every sigma is below 1. Components become 4-5 times
   narrower, while the means do not move. It is the sampling setting, not learned behaviour. The official
   config uses tau=2, so this is not a bug in our code. We have not established the authors' rationale.

9. Is there a trade-off?
   Yes, but limited. tau=2 scores better on full-scanpath dispersion, slightly on direction concentration, on
   MultiMatch position, and has no off-image fixations (tau=1: 0.2-0.4%, handled by clipping). tau=1 scores better
   on amplitude, revisit rate, the amplitude and revisit group differences, and MultiMatch direction.
   Several tau=2 gains are explained by compensation for excess fixations, or by a measure that rewards tightness.
   Temporal metrics are unaffected either way.

Decision. tau=1 is supported by several metrics rather than one: amplitude, revisit, two group differences, and
its identity with the distribution scored by NLL. No single metric decided this.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E. SAFE AND UNSAFE CLAIMS

Safe
- In this code, tau raises each sigma to the power tau; tau=2 narrowed all components and durations are not tempered.
- On validation images, tau=2 shortened saccades and raised revisit rate compared with tau=1.
- tau=1 better matched real saccade amplitude (clearly for ASD) and revisit rate.
- Temperature did not reduce fixation overproduction.
Not safe
- "Lower temperature improves the model" or "the likelihood improved". Weights and NLL did not change.
- "tau=1 is realistic". Fixation count, total time, direction concentration and several group differences remain wrong.
- Any claim that tau=1 is better on independent data. It has not been tested there.
- Reading MultiMatch p-values as large effects, or crediting the model for window-matched improvements.
- Any clinical or diagnostic conclusion.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F. LIMITATIONS

- Validation images were used to choose the Phase-2 checkpoint, so this split is not independent of the model.
- Only tau = 1 and 2 were tested, as requested. Dispersion lies between them, but intermediate values are untested.
- Few real scanpaths per image (ASD 4-13, TD 8-14), which adds noise to per-image error.
- No participant IDs across images, so uncertainty covers images and seeds, not participants.
- Group contrasts are image-level, with no adjustment for age or other covariates.
- Earlier tau=2 results (including Gaze4ASD) remain valid as tau=2 results and were not changed. Their "too small"
  spatial scale may partly reflect this setting. Whether that holds on Gaze4ASD was not tested here, by design.
- Window matching and the bias comparison are post hoc.

HOW TEMPERATURE IS IMPLEMENTED
File: tppgaze/tppgaze/distributions/mixture_same_family.py, line 48:
    scales = temperature * log_scales if temperature is not None else log_scales
Then component scale = exp(scales). So, for each mixture component and axis:

    sigma_tau = exp(tau * log sigma) = sigma ^ tau

Means (locs) and mixture weights do not change. Log scales are clamped to [-5, 3] before this step.
- tau = 1 is the original distribution, numerically identical to no temperature.
- tau = 2 gives sigma squared. This is NOT the usual "higher temperature = more random".
  It narrows a component when sigma < 1 and would widen one when sigma > 1.
- In this model 100% of the mixture weight has sigma < 1 (median RMS sigma 0.131, normalized coordinates).
  So tau = 2 narrows every component here.

Spatial sampling: directly affected.
Duration sampling: NOT directly affected. sample_conditioned calls get_inter_time_dist(next_context),
which passes temperature None, and LogNormalMixture ignores its temperature argument.
Duration can change only indirectly: tempered positions enter the history, which changes later contexts.

Official code notes
- TPPGaze.sample() has a temperature argument but ignores it and uses self.temperature (line 101).
- tppgaze/data/config.yaml sets temperature: 2, so tau = 2 is the official configured default.
- training/generate_cond.py passes its temperature argument through, so both settings really apply.
- Loss and NLL use temperature None.

CHECKS BEFORE AND DURING GENERATION (the run aborts if a gate fails)
- tau = 1 scale identical to no temperature: passed, exact.
- tau = 2 scale equals sigma squared: max relative error 3.5e-07.
- Means and weights unchanged by temperature: passed, exact.
- Duration distribution ignores a temperature argument: passed, exact.
- Validation joint NLL reproduced: -0.59640 (known best validation -0.5964).
- Same seed reruns identical: passed.
- First fixation duration identical across temperatures for the same seed: max difference 0.
  This proves the pairing works and that temperature cannot affect the first duration.

GENERATION
Existing sample_conditioned; 3 s window; 20 scanpaths per image per condition; batch of 20, one image and
one condition per batch, so batch composition is identical across temperatures.
10 seeds: torch.manual_seed(100000*(rep+1)+1000*(image_rank+1)+group_id). The same seed is used at both
temperatures, so differences are paired. 36,000 scanpaths; 20 had no fixation inside the window.
Longest sequence 27 steps (limit 80, never reached).
Saved in generation_tau1/ and generation_tau2/, one file per seed, unclipped.

COORDINATES
Primary: pixel coordinates clipped to the image, as the official generate_predictions does. Clipping is
applied only for measurement and never enters the sampler's history.
Sensitivity: unclipped, as in our earlier analyses. Off-image fixations are also reported.
Chosen before seeing results.

METRICS
training/metrics.py and metric_row in eval_external_v2.py. Saccade amplitude and dispersion divided by the
image diagonal. Revisit radius 5% of the diagonal. Rate = fixations / summed fixation time.
Direction: 12-bin direction distribution, horizontal fraction, and mean resultant length (concentration).
Distances to real (same group, same image): Wasserstein-1 for durations, counts, amplitudes, dispersion,
revisit rates and positions (mean of x and y, /diagonal); total variation for direction.
MultiMatch: own implementation, no simplification, at most 30 pairs per image and comparison. Clipped only.
Pair selection does not depend on temperature.

STATISTICS (exploratory)
Unit: image (n = 45), one value per image pooled over 10 seeds, paired across temperatures.
Percentile bootstrap and sign-flip permutation, 10,000 each. Holm within each family and analysis.
Seed variation: the same comparison per seed (mean, SD, range, number of seeds favouring each side).
Sampling-noise reference: split-half within one temperature (seeds 1-5 vs 6-10), and SD of seed means.
Group differences: per-image ASD - TD. Saliency4ASD records have no participant IDs across images, so no
participant-level analysis is possible here.
Absolute-gap intervals are bootstrap intervals of a difference of absolute values, and can sit off-centre.

POST HOC, ADDED AFTER SEEING RESULTS
- Grand-mean absolute bias comparison.
- Window-matched metrics: generated scanpaths truncated to fixations with onset before the mean real
  fixation time for that group and image. This removes the excess fixations, but it uses real timing,
  so it is a diagnostic, not something the model does.
- Tempered position NLL on real validation fixations was planned before generation and is a diagnostic only.

GAZE4ASD BEHAVIOURAL EVALUATION AT TEMPERATURE 1.0 - FINAL REPORT

Frozen Phase-2 model, corrected Gaze4ASD (fit-to-height, Policy A, partly inferred mapping), 30 images, 10 seeds.
This evaluates generated behaviour, NOT a change in model likelihood. NLL was not rerun; it remains -0.5396.
No clinical or diagnostic validity is claimed.

BOTTOM LINE
tau = 1 clearly improves external spatial realism, and the two validation improvements transfer: per-image error
falls by about half for saccade amplitude and by 57-66% for revisit rate. All four prespecified tests pass.
It does not make generated behaviour realistic overall. Temporal behaviour is unchanged, fixation
overproduction remains, direction concentration gets slightly worse, and the group differences that were wrong
at tau = 2 are still wrong. The correct spatial contrasts grow but remain well below real size.

E. ANSWERS

1. Did tau = 1 improve external behavioural similarity?
   Yes for spatial behaviour, clearly. No for temporal behaviour. Direction concentration is slightly worse.
   Overall it is a substantial but partial improvement, and generated behaviour is still measurably unlike real.

2. Which metrics improved?
   Saccade amplitude and revisit rate (prespecified). Exploratory: dispersion, horizontal fraction, and distributions
   of positions and directions. Spatial contrasts between groups (amplitude, dispersion, revisit).

3. Which did not?
   Unchanged: fixation duration, count, rate, total time, MultiMatch duration. Slightly worse: direction concentration.
   Mixed: other MultiMatch components. Per-image correspondence of contrasts is no better.

4. Does the ASD-vs-TD contrast better match real Gaze4ASD?
   In magnitude for three spatial effects, by small amounts: 50%, 60% and 29% of real, against 33%, 46% and 15%. These
   remain too small. Directions are no better: concentration, total time and count remain wrong.

5. Does fixation-count overproduction remain?
   Yes, unchanged: 10.24 and 9.62 against real 6.25 and 6.41. Excess within the real window: +1.23 and +1.00.
   Total fixation time is also too long (2.79, 2.77 vs 1.99, 2.07). Temperature cannot fix this.

6. Are the improvements consistent across seeds?
   Yes. All prespecified tests, and dispersion and horizontal fraction, favour tau = 1 in 10/10 seeds and both groups.
   Temporal differences are noise. Contrast magnitude varies by seed, so use pooled values.

7. Which findings are robust?
   - tau = 1 reduces amplitude, dispersion and horizontal-fraction error: full scanpaths, window-matched,
     clipped and unclipped, all seeds, both groups.
   - tau = 1 reduces ASD revisit error under every check. TD revisit is sensitive to fixation count.
   - Amplitude and revisit improvements occur on both Saliency4ASD validation and Gaze4ASD.
   - Temperature does not change temporal behaviour or fixation overproduction.
   - tau = 1 worsens direction concentration slightly, on both datasets.
   - Group contrasts in the wrong direction stay wrong.

8. What should we report in the paper?
   Report:
   - tau = 1 was selected on Saliency4ASD validation, then evaluated once on Gaze4ASD with paired seeds.
   - The formula: tau raises each sigma to the power tau, so tau = 2 narrows; durations are not tempered.
   - The amplitude and revisit error reductions, with absolute values and intervals.
   - The remaining errors: fixation count, total time, durations, concentration, and contrasts at 29-60% of real.
   - The TD revisit caveat, and three wrong-direction contrasts.
   - That likelihood is not affected, and that conditioning did not improve external NLL (earlier comparison).
   Do not say:
   - "tau = 1 improves the model" or its likelihood.
   - "Generated scanpaths are realistic", or that they reproduce ASD/TD group differences in general.
   - "Validated" or "confirmed" without the limits below, or any clinical implication.
   Do not present validation dispersion as a tau = 2 advantage, or external dispersion as prespecified.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F. CONFIRMATORY VS EXPLORATORY

Prespecified (four tests, script header fixed before generation): closest to confirmatory. Not formally preregistered.
We had already seen tau = 2 on Gaze4ASD, and narrowing follows from the formula, so this is less independent than a
fresh confirmation. Describe them as prespecified directional tests.
Exploratory: everything else, including all group contrasts, MultiMatch, distances and window matching.

LIMITATIONS
- Gaze4ASD is independent in participants and images, but the authors' stimulus selection appears to have used
  Saliency4ASD. Stimuli are selected for group differences and are mostly faces.
- Real spatial values depend on a partly inferred mapping; only this mapping was evaluated here.
- Only tau = 1 and 2 were compared. Uncertainty is over these 30 images and 10 seeds; generated data have no participants.
- Phase 1 was not run at tau = 1, so the earlier Phase 1 vs Phase 2 behavioural comparison applies to tau = 2 only.
- Groups are unbalanced (33 vs 133), and age is not adjusted.
- MultiMatch is our own implementation, with a high ceiling and limited discrimination.
- Earlier tau = 2 results are unchanged and remain valid as tau = 2 results.
- The run stopped twice at a gate because of rounding in saved data; see methodology_notes.txt.

METHODOLOGY NOTES - GAZE4ASD BEHAVIOURAL EVALUATION AT TEMPERATURE 1.0

WHAT THIS IS
Generated behaviour only. No training, no temperature tuning, and no NLL evaluation. Temperature affects
sampling, not the model or its likelihood. Corrected Gaze4ASD Phase-2 NLL remains -0.5396.
tau = 1 was chosen from Saliency4ASD validation images before any tau = 1 result existed on Gaze4ASD.

MODEL AND DATA
Checkpoint: training/runs/phase2_embedding_only/best_model_conditioned.pth, frozen, evaluation mode.
sha256 35319673382378487d23c1871cab0cf05b71b1e7963e180ec024e5d80901a9a4, unchanged after the run.
Data: external_data/Gaze4ASD_processed_v2/fit_height_policy_a/gaze4asd_fit_height_policy_a.json.
33 ASD and 133 TD participants, 771 and 3,605 trials, 30 stimuli. Mapping unchanged: fit-to-height, strongly
supported but partly inferred, NOT experimentally confirmed.
Conditions: ASD = 0, TD = 1. Normalization: Saliency4ASD train statistics from summary_full.json
(mean -1.4365682299606377, std 0.6936114719391417).

GENERATION
Existing sample_conditioned, temperature 1.0, 3 s window, 20 samples per condition per image, batch of 20,
30 images, 10 seeds. All other settings match the tau = 2 run.

SEEDS: FULLY PAIRED
tau = 2 scanpaths were recovered from phase1_vs_phase2/raw/generated_scanpaths.json.gz and not regenerated
or modified. Seed rule: 100000*(rep+1)+1000*(image_rank+1)+group_id, the same at both temperatures.
Pairing was verified, not assumed:
- two saved tau = 2 sets regenerated exactly (max difference 0);
- first fixation duration identical to the saved tau = 2 run for all scanpaths (max difference 0);
- the same scanpaths are empty at both temperatures (11 of 12,000 each).

GATE FAILURES DURING THE RUN (disclosed)
The run stopped twice at the gate comparing recomputed tau = 2 metrics with the saved summaries. No
results existed yet at either stop. Logs: raw/attempt1_failed_gate_log.txt, raw/attempt2_failed_gate_log.txt.
Attempt 1: cell-level tolerance of 1e-3 was exceeded by revisit rate; that log's per-metric dictionary was
  keyed by metric only, so TD values overwrote ASD values.
Attempt 2: 6 of 1,200 cells exceeded the tolerance, in revisit rate (1) and horizontal fraction (5).
Cause: saved scanpaths hold 5 decimals, and both metrics use a threshold, so a borderline saccade or
fixation can flip. All six sets were regenerated at full precision and matched the saved summaries exactly;
they differ only after rounding. The largest grand-mean effect is 2e-5.
Final gate: grand-mean difference at most 1e-4 for all metrics; no cell over 1e-3 in continuous metrics; at
most 12 threshold flips. Result: 6 flips. tau = 1 and tau = 2 metrics are both computed from values rounded
to 5 decimals by the same code, so the comparison is not affected.
Partial output folders were removed before each rerun; they held only tau = 1 seed files and logs.

COORDINATES
Primary: clipped to the image, as in the validation experiment and the official generate_predictions.
Sensitivity: unclipped, as in the earlier tau = 2 Gaze4ASD analysis. Tau = 2 values here can differ from
earlier reports in the fourth decimal because of this clipping.

METRICS
training/metrics.py and metric_row in eval_external_v2.py. Amplitude and dispersion divided by the image
diagonal. Revisit radius 5% of the diagonal. Rate = fixations / summed fixation time.
Direction: 12-bin direction distribution, horizontal fraction, and mean resultant length (concentration).
The circular mean of direction is descriptive only, as pooled directions are close to uniform.
Distances to real (same group, same image): Wasserstein-1, total variation for direction.
MultiMatch: own implementation, no simplification, at most 30 pairs per image and comparison. Clipped only.
Pair selection does not depend on temperature. Tau = 2 MultiMatch was recomputed on these pairs from saved
scanpaths, so it differs slightly from the earlier values, which used other pairs and unclipped coordinates.

STATISTICS
Unit: image (n = 30), one value per image pooled over 10 seeds, paired across temperatures.
Percentile bootstrap and sign-flip permutation, 10,000 each. Holm within each family.
Seed variation: the same comparison per seed. Sampling-noise reference: split-half and SD of seed means.
Real ASD vs TD at participant level: read from the saved statistics/participant_level_real.csv, not recomputed.
Absolute-gap intervals are bootstrap intervals of a difference of absolute values, and can sit off-centre.

PRESPECIFIED BEFORE GENERATION (fixed in the script header; not formally preregistered)
Four tests from the validation report: per-image error for saccade amplitude and revisit rate, ASD and TD, with
tau = 1 expected to be closer. Holm across these four.
Transfer criterion per test: tau = 1 closer, image-level 95% CI excludes zero, at least 8 of 10 seeds agree.
Caveat: we had already seen tau = 2 results on Gaze4ASD, including spatial scale that was too small and
revisit that was too high, and the narrowing at tau = 2 follows from the formula. So these are prespecified
directional tests, not fully independent confirmation.
Everything else is exploratory, including window matching, which was planned before this run because of the
validation result.
