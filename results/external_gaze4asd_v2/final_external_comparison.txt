E. ANSWERS

1. Does conditioning improve external NLL?
   No. Overall it is worse: -0.0172 nats per fixation (-3.1%), and the interval excludes zero under
   all three resampling schemes. The difference is small but goes against Phase 2.

2. ASD, TD, or both?
   ASD gains a little: +0.0104 (+5.4%), clear of zero by participants but not by images. TD is worse:
   -0.0229 (-3.6%), clear of zero under all schemes. Moreover, the ASD gain comes from an embedding
   that also helps TD, so it does not show ASD-specific modelling.

3. Spatial prediction?
   Not improved. ASD +0.0075 (interval includes zero under every scheme). TD -0.0160 (excludes zero
   by participants, includes zero by images). There is a small group-specific spatial signal
   (right vs wrong label +0.0130), but it does not produce a net gain over Phase 1.

4. Duration prediction?
   ASD +0.0029 and TD -0.0069. Both are clear of zero under all schemes and both are very small. TD
   duration is worse on 29 of 30 images. Right vs wrong label is zero (-0.0001), so duration has no
   group-specific signal. The two embeddings simply differ in overall quality.

5. Does Phase 2 reproduce real ASD/TD differences better than unconditioned Phase 1?
   Partly, on 2 of 10 measures with intervals excluding zero: dispersion and saccade amplitude, which
   reach 46% and 33% of the real difference. Phase 1 can show none by construction, so this is a low
   bar. It gets worse on fixation count and total fixation time, whose directions are wrong.

6. Which behavioural metrics improve?
   Match to real, after Holm: only TD total fixation time, by 2.6%. TD fixation count is borderline
   (+7.7%, Holm .052). TD rate is nominal only (+15.8%, Holm .257).
   Group contrast: dispersion and amplitude. MultiMatch: TD position and TD shape, both under 0.01.

7. Which remain problematic?
   Made worse by Phase 2 (Holm < .05): ASD fixation count, ASD window count, ASD rate, TD saccade
   amplitude, TD dispersion, TD revisit rate.
   Wrong in both models: fixation count (about 10 vs 6), total time (2.8 vs 2.0 s, sampler fills 3 s),
   amplitude and dispersion too small, revisit too high, horizontal fraction .46 vs .56, direction
   concentration too low, MultiMatch duration about .06 below real-vs-real.
   Group contrast in the wrong direction: count, total time, direction concentration.

8. Robust across images, participants and seeds?
   Robust under all three, in Phase 1's favour: TD joint and duration loss; overall joint and
   duration loss (overall joint only just: both-resampled upper bound -0.0007).
   Robust in Phase 2's favour: ASD duration gain, which is tiny.
   Generated metrics can only be resampled by image and seed, because generated scanpaths have no
   participants. Robust on both: dispersion and amplitude contrast (10/10 seeds), with the TD caveat;
   TD amplitude and dispersion match worse (0/10 seeds favour Phase 2).
   Not robust: ASD joint gain (participants only; 17/30 images); ASD position gain; TD and overall
   position loss (participants only).
   Single 20-sample runs vary widely, e.g. revisit difference -.0345 to +.0177 across seeds.

9. What can we safely say in the paper?
   Safe:
   - On Gaze4ASD, unconditioned Phase 1 had lower overall NLL than Phase 2 (-0.557 vs -0.540).
   - The small internal gain from group conditioning did not transfer externally.
   - Phase 2 scored ASD trials slightly better and TD trials slightly worse than Phase 1.
   - Conditioned generation showed dispersion and saccade amplitude contrasts in the real direction,
     at about 46% and 33% of real size, accompanied by a worse match to real TD.
   - Conditioning did not fix fixation count, revisit rate or spatial scale; some measures reversed.
   - The learned embedding carried a small position-related group signal externally (post hoc).
   Not safe:
   - "Conditioning improves external prediction" or "generalises".
   - "Phase 2 better captures ASD gaze" based on the +0.0104 ASD gain.
   - "Phase 2 generates realistic group-specific scanpaths."
   - Any headline percentage without the absolute value; any p-value as confirmatory.
   - Why TD worsened: no cause has been tested.
   - Any clinical, diagnostic or screening use.
