4. QUANTIZATION AROUND 8.33 ms (TOBII T120 SAMPLING RATE)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Tobii T120 eye tracker samples gaze at exactly 120 Hz.
Theoretical frame interval: Delta_t = 1000 ms / 120 = 8.333333... ms.

Mathematical Verification:
Let k be the integer number of video frames. Theoretical duration in milliseconds:
   D_theo(k) = round(k * 1000 / 120)

Empirical Findings:
1. All 63,627 duration values are strict integers (zero non-integer floats exist in the dataset).
2. Exactly 49,666 out of 63,627 fixations (78.06%) perfectly match round(k * 1000 / 120).
3. The maximum deviation from an exact multiple of 8.333 ms across the entire dataset is only 4.00 ms.
4. The mean absolute deviation across all fixations is 0.3284 ms.
5. 100% of duration values fall within [k * 8.333 - 4.0, k * 8.333 + 4.0] ms.

6. DOES DURATION REPRESENT FIXATION DURATION OR ANOTHER TIMING QUANTITY?
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The investigation reveals that while duration represents fixation dwell time nominally, it suffers from
several severe processing anomalies:

1. Nominal Nature: It is NOT saccade duration, nor cumulative session timestamp. It is an event dwell time.
2. Lack of Minimum Duration Filtering (Raw Sample Leakage):
   - 3,936 fixations (6.19%) have a duration of exactly 8 ms (1 frame).
   - Standard fixation filters discard events shorter than 60–100 ms because human visual processing cannot
     occur in 8 ms. Their inclusion proves the authors exported unfiltered or minimally filtered raw event data.
3. Cross-Trial / Grey-Screen Leakage (Multi-Second Fixations):
   - Over 125 fixations exceed 3,000 ms (up to 11,483 ms!).
   - Investigation reveals identical extreme durations (e.g. 11,483 ms, 9,102 ms) occurring across different
     images at screen center (x=602, y=542 and x=590, y=549 on a 1280x1024 monitor).
   - Mechanism: When Tobii Studio records a continuous session of 30 images, if a participant maintained
     fixation on the center cross during the 1-second grey interval or across consecutive image changes,
     the software maintained the fixation object across stimulus markers. When sliced per image, the full
     cumulative duration of the multi-trial fixation was assigned to that image!
4. Saccadic / Blink Gaps:
   - Summing fixation durations within a segment frequently totals >3,000 ms (38.01% of segments) even though
     the image was only on screen for 3,000 ms. This confirms fixation events extended into the grey interval.

RECOMMENDED PREPROCESSING / FILTERING PIPELINE FOR RESEARCHERS:
1. Minimum Duration Threshold: Remove all fixations with duration < 60 ms or < 80 ms (eliminating ~10-14%
   of data that represents 1-frame tracker noise and saccade artifacts).
2. Maximum Duration Clamping: Cap individual fixations at 1,500 ms, or discard fixations > 3,000 ms.
3. Segment Timeline Truncation: Truncate cumulative scanpath time at 3,000 ms to discard fixations that leaked
   into the 1-second inter-stimulus grey interval.
4. Discard Spurious Segments: Drop participant segments where total fixations < 2 or total duration < 300 ms
   (where the participant was not attending to the stimulus).
